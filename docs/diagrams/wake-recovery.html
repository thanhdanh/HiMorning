A system Stop is not completion

Silencing the iOS alert leaves the finite backup plan unresolved until completion or delayed self-confirmation.

Scope: one enabled occurrence, seven states. Scheduling capacity failures, permission changes and overlapping alarms stay in the device checklist. “No further backups” does not mean an already-ringing system alert is forcibly stopped. App opening on system dismissal is not guaranteed.
