## What we checked

`manifest.json` maps each unmodified XCTest attachment to its source case and result bundle. These images use synthetic test fixtures, including completion dates and alarm schedules; they are not records from seven days of user research. The completion screenshot exercises the non-rehearsal path, but does not establish a physical alarm ringing.

A separate manual session on preserved non-fixture simulator data observed a scheduled alarm ring, route to the matching mission, and advance the newly enrolled journey from zero to one. That narrower observation is documented in [the experience review](../../FIRST-MORNING-REVIEW.md); it is not substituted by the fixture screenshots.

## Limits and next steps

The screenshots demonstrate rendered product states. They do not establish long-term retention, willingness to pay, fitness effects or physical-device overnight reliability. Earlier commerce and first-use-only evidence was moved outside this worktree after the user clarified the product goal.
