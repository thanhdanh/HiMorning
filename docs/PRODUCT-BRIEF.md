# A small, useful start

HiMorning aims to make the first action after an alarm clear and manageable,
with time and a chosen challenge at the center of the experience.

**Status:** Product direction and hypotheses, updated 22 October 2026. Implemented
features are listed in the [repository README](../README.md#implemented-pilot);
usefulness, retention and comparative advantage are not measured.
**Audience:** Product, design and engineering.

## At a glance

| Goal | Product implication |
|---|---|
| Set an alarm quickly | Current-time picker, one-time default, compact repeat days and secondary label/sound settings |
| Make the task understandable | Preview through a confirmed trial; show only relevant instructions and observed camera guides |
| Keep feedback fair | Generous calibrated detection, no partial/duplicate counts, short infrequent speech and a clear recovery path |
| Fit different schedules and rooms | Time-neutral completion copy, camera-free reading choices and explicit device permissions |
| Preserve a distinct identity | HiMorning yellow, warm surfaces, balanced cards and restrained motion/glass |
| Keep basic alarms dependable | Local scheduling/recovery independent of network or purchases |

Completing a task does not prove wakefulness, exercise safety or a learning gain.
System Stop can silence one ring; the finite backup plan is assistance, not an
unbreakable exercise requirement.

## Hypotheses to test

- A brief, familiar task is useful across repeated mornings after novelty fades.
- Trying a task before saving reduces uncertainty without making setup slower.
- Quiet reading choices and usable close-camera framing fit more real rooms.
- Fair detection and calmer audio reduce frustration more than a larger catalog.
- Optional progress and a no-card preview create enough value for voluntary Pro.

Use a small consented pilot across different schedules, spaces and abilities.
Record task/version, start-to-finish time, false counts, unnecessary rejections,
recovery and reasons for switching. Report denominators, missed observations and
novelty effects. Do not infer a competitor's reliability from store listings or
make a head-to-head claim without a disclosed comparison.

## Boundaries

The current [catalog](MODE-CHALLENGES.md) already includes reading, riddles and
several movements. Free-form dance scoring, combined Speak & Choose, QR, math,
technique coaching and referral rewards remain separate possible experiments.
Do not copy old feature-priority scores or model choices into a current roadmap.

Keep raw camera/audio and recognized speech out of product telemetry by default.
Any research or analytics collection requires its own defined consent and handling.
An app session ending quickly can be success: helping the person leave the phone
is part of the intended experience.

## What we checked

Current direction incorporates implemented behavior and iterative iPhone feedback.
No new user study or competitor evaluation was performed for this review.
