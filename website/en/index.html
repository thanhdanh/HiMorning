Privacy policy

Updated 15 November 2026

Data on your iPhone

HiMorning stores alarm settings, challenge choices, and routine progress on your iPhone. It keeps up to 31 days of activity history and may use the iOS Keychain to store dates used to determine preview access. These features do not require a HiMorning account.

Camera and microphone

Movement challenges need the camera. Read Aloud needs the microphone. HiMorning processes images and sound on your iPhone to assess the challenge. The app does not save or upload camera video, images, or audio recordings. iOS may download language assets to enable on-device speech recognition. You can turn off access in iPhone Settings or choose a different challenge.

Optional ads

HiMorning uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform to manage ad privacy choices. Google’s consent tool may check your choices when the app opens. HiMorning requests rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one and your privacy choices allow the request. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash logs, performance information, and diagnostics. These data can be used to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

HiMorning requests non-personalized ads. The app does not send camera images, voice recordings, or morning history to AdMob. When available, you can change your choices under HiMorning Pro → Privacy policy → Ad privacy choices.

HiMorning Pro

Apple handles payment and subscription management. HiMorning uses verified transaction information from StoreKit to determine Pro access. The app does not receive your card number or wallet details.

Apple Privacy Policy

Retention, deletion, and contact

You can delete individual alarms in the app. When you uninstall HiMorning, iOS removes its local app container. iOS and Apple manage Keychain data, backups, and purchase history separately. Google handles advertising data under its privacy policy.

When you email support, we use your email address and message to respond and resolve your request. You can contact us with privacy questions, to request access to or deletion of support data, or to withdraw consent.
