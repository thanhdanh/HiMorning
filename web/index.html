Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 90-minute sleep cycle calculator work?

Sleep progresses in ~90-minute cycles. This calculator counts 4 to 6 full cycles and includes an average 15-minute buffer to fall asleep so your alarm lands near lighter sleep.

Does HiMorning upload my camera or microphone data?

No. Movement counting and Read Aloud checks run locally on your iPhone with Apple Vision and on-device speech frameworks.
